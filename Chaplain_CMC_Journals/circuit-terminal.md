An engineering blueprint for interfacing an organic vine conductor with a physical terminal resistor must manage the transition from ionic fluid transport (inside the plant tissue) to electronic conduction (inside a carbon/charcoal substrate).

Below is the technical specification and mechanical schematic for this interface node.

* * * * *

📋 Technical Specifications & Component List
--------------------------------------------

-   The Conductor (Live Vine Trunk): A mature, non-woody vine segment (*Vitis* or similar climbing liana) continuously hydrated via an internal sap stream enriched with copper sulfate or natron salts. Ions function as the primary charge carriers.
-   The Interface Mesh (Copper Gauze): Fine-mesh wire cloth woven from native copper filigree, wrapped tightly over an exposed, stripped ring of the plant's cambium layer to maximize physical surface contact with the vascular bundle.
-   The Terminal Resistor (Charcoal Hardwood Block): A block of dense, kiln-fired oak or terebinth charcoal. The structural lattice of amorphous carbon acts as a natural semiconductor, introducing predictable electrical resistance into the line.
-   The Dielectric Housing (Bitumen Isolation Block): A protective molding of natural rock asphalt or pine resin that encapsulates the entire connection, preventing the fluid or voltage from grounding out into nearby earth or masonry.

🔧 Step-by-Step Assembly Instructions
-------------------------------------

1\. Preparing the Vascular Node
-------------------------------

Technicians strip away a 5-centimeter band of the outer bark and phloem layers of the vine, exposing the moist, conductive xylem tissue beneath. The cut must be clean to avoid crushing the microscopic micro-tubules carrying the saline sap filament.

2\. Installing the Interface Mesh
---------------------------------

The exposed cambium ring is wrapped securely with the fine copper gauze. Conductive copper wire is coiled tightly over the gauze, compressing it uniformly against the plant tissue. This tight physical seal acts as a transducer, allowing ions traveling in the liquid sap to exchange charge with electrons in the metallic wire.

3\. Calibrating the Charcoal Terminal
-------------------------------------

The outgoing wire from the copper mesh is driven directly into a pre-drilled boring inside the charcoal hardwood block. The length and density of the charcoal block dictate the system's baseline electrical resistance. A longer block increases resistance (attenuating the signal), while a shorter block decreases it.

4\. Sealing and Environmental Isolation
---------------------------------------

The completed assembly is suspended inside a dry stone junction recess. Melted bitumen or liquid tree resin is poured over the entire interface point, completely encapsulating the wire-to-vine and wire-to-charcoal connections. This protective shell hardens to lock out humidity, prevent oxygen from oxidizing the copper, and block external current leaks.
